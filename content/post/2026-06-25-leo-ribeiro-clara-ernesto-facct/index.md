[Leo Sampaio Ferraz Ribeiro](/people/leo-sampaio-ferraz-ribeiro/) and undergraduate researcher [Clara Ernesto de Carvalho](/people/clara-ernesto-de-carvalho/) published a paper at the 2026 ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency (FAccT), with co-authors Carlos Caetano, Sandra Avila, João Macedo, and Camila Laranjeira.

The paper, “Classifying by Proxy: Explainable and Reproducible Ensemble of Proxy Tasks for Child Sexual Abuse Imagery Classification,” presents an ensemble of proxy tasks that improves reproducibility, explainability, and safer distribution of models used to help detect this material.
