[João Neto](/people/joao-batista-neto/) published “Mandible and skull segmentation in cone beam computed tomography using super-voxels and graph clustering” in *The Visual Computer*, with Oscar Cuadros Linares, Jonas Bianchi, Dirceu Raveli, and Bernd Hamann.

The method uses super-voxels and graph clustering to segment mandible and skull structures in cone-beam CT. The publication page is linked below.
