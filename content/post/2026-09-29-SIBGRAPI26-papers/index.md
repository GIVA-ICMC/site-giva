---

GIVA will present two tutorials and eight papers at SIBGRAPI 2026, the 39th Conference on Graphics, Patterns and Images, held from 29 September to 2 October in Goiânia.

Tutorials:

- “An overview of 3D Vision-Language Models,” by [Marcus Costa](/people/marcus-vinicius-lobo-costa/), Vitor Matias, and [Moacir Ponti](/people/moacir-antonelli-ponti/), with collaborators
- “An Introduction to Graph Neural Networks in Computer Vision: A Practical Perspective,” with [Lucas Valem](/people/lucas-pascotti-valem/)

Papers:

- “Region-Aware CLS Token Augmentation for Fine-Grained Image Retrieval,” with Lucas Valem
- “Rank Correlation Meets Gaussian Degree Normalization in GNNs for Image Classification,” with Lucas Valem
- “Rethinking Image Graphs for GCNs: Beyond Similarity with Diversity,” with Lucas Valem
- “ViTs Meet Contrastive Learning for Enhanced Multi-view 3D Shape Features,” with Marcus Costa, [Leo Ribeiro](/people/leo-sampaio-ferraz-ribeiro/), and Moacir Ponti
- “Dataset and Benchmark for Skin Tone Classification in the Wild using the MST Scale,” by Vitor Matias and [João Batista Neto](/people/joao-batista-neto/)
- “Revisiting Temporality for Sketch Representation Learning,” with Moacir Ponti
- “SPLOM-C: Class-centric Visualization of Labeled Multidimensional Data,” with [Maria Oliveira](/people/maria-cristina-ferreira-de-oliveira/)
- “Visual Urban Analytics: From Data Integration to Pattern Uncover,” by Karelia Salinas
