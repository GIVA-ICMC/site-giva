[Leo Ribeiro](/people/leo-sampaio-ferraz-ribeiro/) and [Moacir Ponti](/people/moacir-antonelli-ponti/) published “Scene designer: compositional sketch-based image retrieval with contrastive learning and an auxiliary synthesis task” in *Multimedia Tools and Applications*, with Tu Bui and John Collomosse.

Scene Designer retrieves multi-object scenes from sketches and adds a layout synthesis task to improve the learned representations. The publication page is linked below.
