[Thomas Peron]({{< ref "/people/thomas-peron" >}}) received the São Carlos Science and Technology Prize in the Junior Researcher category for 2024.

The award recognizes his research at ICMC-USP on complex networks, nonlinear dynamics, and data visualization.
