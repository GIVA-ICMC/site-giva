[Moacir Antonelli Ponti]({{< ref "/people/moacir-antonelli-ponti" >}}) and his former PhD student Edresson Casanova received the Best PhD Dissertation Award at PROPOR 2024, the International Conference on Computational Processing of Portuguese.

The prize recognizes Casanova’s doctoral research, supervised by Ponti at GIVA / ICMC-USP, in speech processing and machine learning.
