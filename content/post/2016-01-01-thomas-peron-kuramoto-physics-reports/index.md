[Thomas Peron](/people/thomas-peron/) published “The Kuramoto model in complex networks” in *Physics Reports*, with Francisco A. Rodrigues, Peng Ji, and Jürgen Kurths.

The review covers synchronization of Kuramoto oscillators on complex networks and is one of the most cited papers in the area. The publication page is linked below.
