[Maria Cristina Ferreira de Oliveira](/people/maria-cristina-ferreira-de-oliveira/) and student Diego Silva Cintra received the Best Poster Award at IVAPP 2020 (Information Visualization Theory and Applications), together with co-authors Alan Valejo and Alneu Lopes.

The poster, “Visualization to Assist Interpretation of the Multilevel Paradigm in Bipartite Graphs,” presents visualization methods that help interpret multilevel analysis of bipartite networks.
