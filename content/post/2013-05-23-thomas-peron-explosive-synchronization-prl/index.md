[Thomas Peron](/people/thomas-peron/) published “Cluster Explosive Synchronization in Complex Networks” in *Physical Review Letters*, with Peng Ji, Peter J. Menck, Francisco A. Rodrigues, and Jürgen Kurths.

The paper shows how clusters of oscillators can jump into synchrony in an explosive transition on complex networks. The publication page is linked below.
