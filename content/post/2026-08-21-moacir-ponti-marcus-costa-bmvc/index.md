[Moacir Ponti](/people/moacir-antonelli-ponti/) and [Márcus Costa](/people/marcus-vinicius-lobo-costa/) published a paper at the [37th British Machine Vision Conference](https://bmvc2026.bmva.org/) (BMVC 2026), accepted as a poster on 21 August 2026, with co-authors [Vitor Matias](/people/vitor-pereira-matias/) and Jeová Farias.

The paper, “3D-MRL: Nested Multimodal 3D Representations via Matryoshka Representation Learning,” trains a single 3D encoder to produce nested embeddings that align point clouds with frozen CLIP image and text features. On Objaverse-LVIS, Top-1 accuracy rises from 46.8% to 50.9%. The [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.29285) and [project page](https://usmarcv.github.io/3DMRL_page/) are available online.
