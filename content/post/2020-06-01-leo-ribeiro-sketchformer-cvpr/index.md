[Leo Ribeiro](/people/leo-sampaio-ferraz-ribeiro/) and [Moacir Ponti](/people/moacir-antonelli-ponti/) published “Sketchformer: Transformer-Based Representation for Sketched Structure” at the IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR 2020), with Tu Bui and John Collomosse.

Sketchformer learns a transformer representation of sketch structure for recognition and retrieval. The publication page is linked below.
