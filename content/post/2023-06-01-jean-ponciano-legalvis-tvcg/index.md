[Jean Ponciano](/people/jean-roberto-ponciano/) published “LegalVis: Exploring and Inferring Precedent Citations in Legal Documents” in *IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics*, with Lucas E. Resck, Luis Gustavo Nonato, and Jorge Poco.

LegalVis helps readers explore and infer precedent citations in legal collections. The publication page is linked below.
