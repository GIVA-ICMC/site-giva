[Leo Ribeiro](/people/leo-sampaio-ferraz-ribeiro/) and [Clara Ernesto](/people/clara-ernesto-de-carvalho/) published a paper at the [IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2026) (CVPR 2026) Workshops, with co-authors Carlos Caetano, Camila Laranjeira, Artur Barros, João Macedo, Jefersson A. dos Santos, and Sandra Avila.

The paper, “CSA-Graphs: A Privacy-Preserving Structural Dataset for Child Sexual Abuse Research,” releases scene graphs and skeleton graphs instead of images, so researchers can study CSAI classification without sharing visual content. Combining the two graph types improves performance over either alone. The [open-access PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/CV4CHL/papers/Caetano_CSA-Graphs_A_Privacy-Preserving_Structural_Dataset_for_Child_Sexual_Abuse_Research_CVPRW_2026_paper.pdf) is available from the CVPR workshop pages.
