[Maria Oliveira](/people/maria-cristina-ferreira-de-oliveira/) published “From visual data exploration to visual data mining: A survey” in *IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics*, with Haim Levkowitz.

The survey maps the path from visual data exploration to visual data mining and remains a standard reference in visualization. The publication page is linked below.
