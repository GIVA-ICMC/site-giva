[Lucas Valem](/people/lucas-pascotti-valem/) published “Gait Recognition Based on Deep Learning: A Survey” in *ACM Computing Surveys*, with Claudio Filipi Gonçalves dos Santos, João Paulo Papa, and colleagues.

The survey reviews deep learning methods for identifying people from gait, and organizes the datasets and architectures used in the field. The publication page is linked below.
