[Moacir Antonelli Ponti]({{< ref "/people/moacir-antonelli-ponti" >}}) received the COVID-19 Cough Sub-Challenge Prize at INTERSPEECH 2021, as part of the Stefan Steidl Computational Paralinguistics Award.

The challenge asked teams to detect COVID-19 from cough recordings. The result recognizes GIVA’s work in speech processing and representation learning.
