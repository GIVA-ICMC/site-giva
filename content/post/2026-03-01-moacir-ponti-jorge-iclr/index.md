[Moacir Antonelli Ponti](/people/moacir-antonelli-ponti/) and Jorge Franco published a paper at ICLR 2026, the Fourteenth International Conference on Learning Representations, with co-authors Gabriel Duarte, Alexander Nikitin, Diego Mesquita, and Amauri Souza.

The paper, “Differentiable Lifting for Topological Neural Networks,” introduces DiffLift (∂lift), a learnable lifting from graphs to higher-order complexes that can be trained end-to-end with topological neural networks. An earlier version also appeared as a poster at a NeurIPS 2025 workshop. The publication page is linked below.
