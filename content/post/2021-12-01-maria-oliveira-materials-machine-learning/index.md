[Maria Oliveira](/people/maria-cristina-ferreira-de-oliveira/) published “Big data and machine learning for materials science” in *Discover Materials*, with Jose F. Rodrigues, Larisa Florea, Dermot Diamond, and Osvaldo N. Oliveira.

The review covers how large datasets and machine learning accelerate materials discovery and chemical sensing, and discusses limits of the approach. The publication page is linked below.
