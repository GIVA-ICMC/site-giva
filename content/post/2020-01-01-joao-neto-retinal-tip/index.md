[João Neto](/people/joao-batista-neto/) published “Segmenting Cellular Retinal Images by Optimizing Super-Pixels, Multi-Level Modularity, and Cell Boundary Representation” in *IEEE Transactions on Image Processing*, with Oscar Cuadros Linares and Bernd Hamann.

The paper combines super-pixels, multi-level modularity, and cell-boundary cues to segment cellular retinal images. The publication page is linked below.
