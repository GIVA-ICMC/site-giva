[Jean Ponciano](/people/jean-roberto-ponciano/) published “LargeNetVis: Visual Exploration of Large Temporal Networks Based On Community Taxonomies” in *IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics*, with Claudio D. G. Linhares, Diogenes S. Pedro, Luis E. C. Rocha, Agma J. M. Traina, and Jorge Poco.

The system uses community taxonomies to make large temporal networks readable. The publication page is linked below.
