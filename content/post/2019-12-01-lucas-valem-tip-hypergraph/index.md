[Lucas Valem](/people/lucas-pascotti-valem/) published “Multimedia Retrieval Through Unsupervised Hypergraph-Based Manifold Ranking” in *IEEE Transactions on Image Processing*, with Daniel Carlos Guimarães Pedronette, Jurandy Almeida, and Ricardo da S. Torres.

The paper ranks multimedia items with an unsupervised hypergraph manifold method, without labeled training data. The publication page is linked below.
