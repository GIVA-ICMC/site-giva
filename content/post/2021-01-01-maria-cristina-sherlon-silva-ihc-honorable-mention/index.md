[Maria Cristina Ferreira de Oliveira](/people/maria-cristina-ferreira-de-oliveira/) and student Sherlon da Silva received the Research Track Honorable Mention Award at IHC 2021, the 20th Brazilian Symposium on Human Factors in Computing Systems, with co-author Evangelos Milios (Dalhousie University).

The paper, “Evaluating visual analytics for text information retrieval,” studies how visual analytics can support finding relevant information in document collections. A later journal version of this work is linked below.
