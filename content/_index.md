  GIVA is a research group at ICMC-USP working on graphics, images, visualization, and analytics.
  We study visualization applications in data analysis — image collections, document collections,
  music collections, social networks, biosensor measurements, volumes from computational simulations,
  and multidimensional datasets — as well as computational geometry and geometric processing,
  image processing and pattern recognition, validation and evaluation of visualization techniques,
  visualization and visual data mining, and visualization in data science and Big Data.
